NHS 111 ONLINE CHECK

You can answer "Not sure" to any question, or skip it.

1. EMERGENCY CHECK
Is any of this happening right now, or has it happened today? Tick all that apply.
[ ] Pain, tightness, pressure or a heavy feeling in your chest
[ ] Your face dropping on one side, an arm going weak or heavy, or slurred or muddled speech, even if it has now passed
[ ] Struggling to breathe, or too breathless to finish a sentence
[ ] Bleeding that is still pouring or spurting after pressing on it for 10 minutes
[ ] Passing out, having a fit, being very hard to wake, or not making sense
[ ] Spots or bruise-like marks that stay visible when you press a clear glass firmly on them
